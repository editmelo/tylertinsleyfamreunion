Who We Are

One Family. Many Branches.

We are the Tyler-Tinsley family: generations of aunties, uncles, cousins, grandbabies and the elders who started it all. Our roots run deep in Indiana, and over the years our branches have stretched across the country from coast to coast.

Every reunion is our chance to come back to the table. We tell the old stories and meet the new babies, and we make sure every generation knows where it comes from. We've been doing this for years, and we're not stopping now.

In June 2027 we're doing it again: a whole weekend of fun and family. Tell your people and save your coins. We'll see you there.
